01The approach

Every garden starts as a drawing.

Tom walks the property, listens to how you use it, and designs the planting, lawn and hardscape together, so they look like one place instead of separate jobs.

  1. 01Walk the property and talk through how you use it
  2. 02Draw the plan: planting, lawn and stonework together
  3. 03Build it, season by season

02Services

Planting to paving.

Six kinds of work, one plan. The planting and the stonework are designed to belong together.

  • 01

    Landscape design

    A plan for the whole property, drawn before anything is planted.

  • 02

    Planting

    Trees, shrubs, perennials and annuals, chosen for Long Island soil and light.

  • 03

    Seed & sod lawns

    New lawns from seed or sod, started on properly prepared ground.

  • 04

    Grading

    Re-shaping the ground so water runs away from the house.

  • 05

    Walks & patios

    Masonry walks and patios, installed to ICPI standards.

  • 06

    Driveways

    Paver driveways that match the rest of the landscape.

04Through the year

The right work in the right season.

Planting, lawns and stonework each have a best time of year. The plan follows the seasons.

Spring

Design consultations, planting, new lawns and spring clean-ups.

Summer

Patios, walks and driveways while the ground is dry and firm.

Fall

Trees and shrubs go in, lawns are seeded, beds are put to rest.
